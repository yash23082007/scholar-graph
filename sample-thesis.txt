Machine Learning Applications in Healthcare

Abstract

Machine learning has transformed healthcare diagnostics and treatment planning.
This paper reviews recent advances in applying deep learning models to medical
imaging, electronic health records, and personalized medicine.

Deep Learning for Medical Imaging

Convolutional neural networks have achieved radiologist-level accuracy in detecting
pneumonia from chest X-rays. The model achieved 94.6% sensitivity and 93.2%
specificity on a test dataset of 50,000 images. Transfer learning from ImageNet
pre-trained models proved effective for medical image classification tasks.

Electronic Health Record Analysis

Natural language processing models extract clinical entities from physician notes
with 89% F1 scores. Predictive models using EHR data can forecast patient
readmission risk with 78% accuracy, enabling early intervention strategies.

Personalized Medicine

Genomic sequencing combined with ML models enables targeted therapy selection.
Studies show that ML-guided treatment plans reduced adverse drug reactions by 35%
and improved patient outcomes in oncology trials.

Conclusion

Machine learning in healthcare shows promise but requires careful validation,
interpretability, and regulatory oversight before clinical deployment.
