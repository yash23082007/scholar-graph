# The Impact of Renewable Energy on Economic Growth

## Introduction

Renewable energy has become a critical driver of economic development worldwide.
This report examines the relationship between renewable energy adoption and
economic growth across multiple countries and time periods.

## Key Findings

Solar and wind energy investments have created over 12 million jobs globally
by 2023. The transition from fossil fuels to renewable sources has reduced
energy costs for consumers while decreasing greenhouse gas emissions.

Countries with early renewable energy adoption experienced GDP growth rates
2.3% higher than those relying on traditional energy sources over a decade.

## Methodology

We analyzed economic data from 45 countries between 2010 and 2023, comparing
renewable energy investment levels with GDP growth, employment rates, and
carbon emission reductions.

## Conclusions

The evidence strongly supports that renewable energy investment correlates
positively with economic growth. Policy recommendations include targeted
subsidies for solar and wind infrastructure, workforce retraining programs,
and international technology transfer agreements.
